# CardGuard — Cards & subscription reminders

Manage linked cards, track trials and get renewal reminders. Your encrypted data stays on your device.

CardGuard helps you see which cards are linked to which services, track trial end dates and prepare for upcoming renewals.

• Identify cards by their last 4 digits without entering a full card number.
• Choose a popular service or add your own.
• View a 30-day agenda and navigate a monthly calendar.
• Resume encrypted drafts after closing the popup; six-character automatic unlock.
• Optionally remember unlock for up to 14 days.
• Readable 16px default text, 18px option, light/dark themes and Vietnamese/English.
• Export/import encrypted backups, with no account, backend or sync.

CardGuard cannot cancel subscriptions or make payments. Removing a card does not cancel a plan. Notifications require Chrome to be running and OS permission. Remembered unlock lets anyone using the same Chrome profile open the vault until expiry; do not enable it on shared devices.
